Always start the server first, then open this URL in your browser:

http://127.0.0.1:8888/TravelMVC/home

The black CMD window shows Tomcat logs. After TravelMVC loads you will see lines starting with [TravelMVC] (servlet output). Each time you open Home, another line appears.

(Double-click Run-TravelMVC-CMD.bat or Start-TravelMVC.bat and keep that window open.)
